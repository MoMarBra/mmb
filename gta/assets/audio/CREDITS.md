## Modifications and original work
All included recordings: mono conversion, high-pass filtering, peak limiting, MP3 encoding; some long recordings trimmed. Designed wash/coffee effects derive from DavidW boiling-water recordings (pitch, filtering and envelopes); not claimed as direct tap or espresso recordings. Source and license mapping for every runtime file: credits.json.
